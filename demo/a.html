Focus mode

A quiet room, a loud set.

Session audio that stays out of the way: no vocals, no ads cutting in between sets. Hover a tile to hear its loop, or click to pin it for the whole session.

Generated loops
The system

Small details, repeated 20,000 times.

A tracker only works if logging is faster than not logging. Every one of these exists to remove a tap from your session.

Two-tap set logging

Weight and reps prefill from your last session. Tap to confirm, hold to edit — the set is saved before you rack the bar.

Routine builder

Push/pull/legs, upper/lower or something entirely your own. Supersets, per-exercise notes and warmup flags included.

Automatic rest timers

The clock starts the moment you finish a set, with a cue that fires on time even if the screen is off.

PR detection

Rep, weight and estimated 1RM records are recognised automatically and surfaced on the exercise you actually lifted.

Volume that makes sense

Weekly tonnage and hard sets per muscle group, not a single meaningless total on a dashboard.

Works offline

Basement gym, no signal. Every set is written locally first and reconciles the moment you are back online.
